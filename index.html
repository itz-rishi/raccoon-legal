Privacy Policy

How information may be handled when Raccoon is used on Discord.

Raccoon · Maintained by Anonymous Devs · Last reviewed: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the kinds of information Raccoon may process to provide its features. The final policy should match the Bot’s actual code, database, logs, hosting setup, and retention practices.

1. Information the Bot may process

Depending on the commands and features used, Raccoon may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command inputs, server configuration, moderation actions, reminders, AFK preferences, premium access status, and operational logs. The precise information depends on which features are enabled and how they are implemented.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to commands, provide server-management and moderation utilities, save server settings, manage authorized access, deliver configured features, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

3. Storage and retention

Information may be stored in configuration files, databases, or operational logs depending on the deployment. Retention periods and deletion methods depend on the actual implementation. Anonymous Devs should publish specific retention periods and deletion procedures once they have been verified against the live system.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, database, or infrastructure providers needed to operate the Bot, or disclosed when required by law. Anonymous Devs does not intend to sell personal information. Only data necessary for operating and supporting the Bot should be accessed or shared.

5. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, Discord tokens, private keys, or other credentials to the Bot or support team.

6. Your choices and data requests

For questions or requests about information associated with your Discord account or server, contact Anonymous Devs through the official support server. Provide enough context to identify the relevant feature or server, but do not share credentials. Requests will be handled according to applicable law and the Bot’s actual capabilities.

7. Children’s privacy

Raccoon is a Discord community tool and is not intended to bypass Discord’s age requirements or applicable child privacy protections.

8. Policy updates

This policy may change when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
